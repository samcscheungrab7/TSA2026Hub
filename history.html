Thaddeus Stevens Society

A museum dedicated to Thaddeus Stevens, one of the most powerful congressmen during and after the Civil War.


Main Street Gettysburg Inc

A nonprofit organization committed to the preservation and revitalization of historic downtown Gettysburg.


Gettysburg Foundation

A 501(c)(3) philanthropic and educational organization working in partnership with the National Park Service to preserve Gettysburg National Military Park.
